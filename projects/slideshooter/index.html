SlideShooter

A vertical 2D space shooter for Android: drag your ship, dodge enemies and take down the boss.

Play now

01 Overview

SlideShooter is a portrait-mode arcade shooter. You slide a finger to move the ship; it fires on its own and turns toward the nearest enemy. Waves of enemies come at you, a boss shows up, power-ups drop, and your score is saved as a high score. I made it to learn GDScript and how a game is put together.

02 Features

  • ControlsTouch-drag to move the ship; shooting and aiming are automatic.
  • EnemiesTwo enemy types and a boss fight.
  • Power-upsCollectible power-ups, activated with a double tap, such as the shield.
  • ProgressThree lives, score, a saved high score and a pause menu.
  • SoundMusic and sound effects, with a mute option.

03 Technologies

Godot 4GDScriptAndroidWebAssembly

It started in Godot 3 in 2020 as my first complete game; in 2024 I began porting it to Godot 4. It now runs in the browser, exported for the web, and plays on a phone.

04 How AI was used

Hand-built: I designed and wrote the game myself in 2020. I started the Godot 4 port myself in 2024; finishing the last few scripts of that port was done later with AI help.
