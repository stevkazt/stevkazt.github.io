Pi-hole

A Pi-hole DNS sinkhole on a Raspberry Pi that blocks ads and trackers for any device that opts in, with no client software.

01 Overview

Pi-hole answers DNS queries and refuses the ones that lead to known ad and tracking domains, which improves browsing and cuts unwanted traffic on every device that uses it.

02 Design decision

I attached the Raspberry Pi to one network segment instead of making it the DNS for the whole network, for three reasons:

  • 1Avoid inspecting other network users' DNS traffic (privacy).
  • 2Let each client opt in to using the Pi-hole.
  • 3Home routers often lack advanced DNS redirection.

03 Implementation

I gave the Pi a static address in /etc/dhcpcd.conf, installed Pi-hole with the official installer, and pointed client devices at it as their DNS resolver.

04 Troubleshooting

At first my laptop saw no blocking. The blocklists needed updating, which sudo pihole -g does.

05 Result

Network-wide ad and tracker blocking without browser extensions, and fewer DNS requests to tracking domains.
